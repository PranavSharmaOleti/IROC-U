# FleetLine E1 — Fleet Feature Specification

*FleetLine E1 — specified for a **200 km/day** depot-charged duty cycle, **45 kWh LFP**.*

---

## Powertrain & Energy — 4 features

| FleetLine E1 feature | Why a fleet operator needs it |
|:--|:--|
| `SPEC` **45 kWh LFP pack, semi-structural CTP** | 200 km/day duty with 10% reserve held at year 8 |
| `SPEC` **PMSM 80 bhp, single-speed reduction drive** | No shift loss across 62,400 km/yr, and no gearset to maintain |
| `SPEC` **Liquid-plate cooling, 48 °C cell design point** | Holds degradation inside the validated band — passive cooling does not |
| `ADD` ⚑ **Per-cell pack SoH telematics, over-the-air** | **`DECISIVE` — THE ONE THAT EARNS THE WARRANTY.** Published LFP studies disagree **3.6×** on time-to-**80%**, so a capacity-retention guarantee is only credible if the pack reports its own degradation |
