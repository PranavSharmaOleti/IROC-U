# IRoC-U — Child of Aries

Micro-UAV proposal for the ISRO Robotics Challenge (IRoC-U 2026), by Team Child of Aries (11043), NIT Durgapur.

## What the project covers

- Radar-assisted SLAM with an FMCW radar (IWR6843) for localisation and 3D mapping in GNSS-denied environments
- ToF-based precision landing on a raised plus-shaped platform
- Wireless inductive charging with adaptive resonant power transfer
- PX4 autopilot on STM32F407 with a Raspberry Pi 4 companion computer
- Section 8 of the proposal breaks the build into workstreams with owners and deadlines

## Status

The build plan and component research are complete. The next milestone is flight-controller hardware; controller-environment work can begin once the FC is available.
